Annual Certification Renewal Deadline

Posted: 15 March 2025

All facilities must complete annual certification renewals by 30 June 2025. Please ensure all candidate records are up to date. Contact your regional office for assistance.

Updated Chemical Hazard Guidelines

Posted: 10 January 2025

The Chemical hazard category certification guidelines have been updated to reflect new regulatory requirements. Updated training materials will be distributed to all regional offices. These guidelines were originally established in 1993 and this is the first major revision since then.
